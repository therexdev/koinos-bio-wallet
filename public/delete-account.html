Delete your Koinos Bio Wallet account and data

Koinos Bio Wallet · usekoinos.com · last updated 6 September 2026

How to request deletion — 3 steps

  1. Move your funds out first if you want to keep them (see the warning below — your wallet is not ours to delete).
  2. Email support@usekoinos.com with the subject Delete my account, and include your Koinos account address — the one at the top of the wallet screen, starting with 1.
  3. We confirm by reply and delete within 30 days.

Email a deletion request

Deleting our records does not delete your wallet.

Your Koinos account is a smart contract on the blockchain, controlled by the passkey on your device. It is not ours to delete, and removing our records does not move, freeze or return any funds. Move your KOIN and NFTs somewhere else first if you want them — otherwise they stay in an account you still control but that we no longer have any record of.

What is deleted, what is kept

Data Outcome Retention
Koinos account address and public key Deleted Within 30 days
Passkey credential IDs (public identifiers only) Deleted Within 30 days
Conversion records — deposit addresses, amounts, transaction hashes Deleted Within 30 days
Encrypted server backups containing the above Aged out automatically Up to 30 further days, then gone
Blockchain transactions (Koinos, Ethereum, Solana) Cannot be deleted Permanent and public

We collect no name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, analytics or advertising identifiers, so there is nothing of that kind to delete. After the backup window above, no copy of your records remains.

Deleting some data without deleting your account

You do not have to close the account to have part of it removed. Email support@usekoinos.com with your Koinos account address and say which of these you want gone — we delete it within 30 days and your wallet keeps working exactly as before:

  • Your conversion history — the deposit addresses, amounts and transaction hashes from the Buy feature, for conversions that have finished. Anything still in progress is kept until it lands, because it is what finishes the job, and is deleted after.
  • A retired passkey credential ID, for a device you no longer use, as long as one passkey remains so you can still sign in.

Email a partial deletion request

The one thing that cannot be removed on its own is your account address and public key: they are what the wallet is. Deleting those is deleting the account, which is the section above.

What cannot be deleted, by anyone

Transactions already recorded on the Koinos, Ethereum or Solana blockchains are permanent and public. No one can remove them — not us, not you, not the people who run those networks. That is how a blockchain works, and it is worth knowing before you start rather than after.

Removing the app from a device

To sign out on one device without deleting anything, open the wallet, go to Security and choose Sign out of this device. That forgets the passkey locally; your account and funds are untouched, and you can sign in again on any device that holds one of your passkeys.
