Privacy Policy

Koinos Bio Wallet · last updated 6 September 2026

This wallet is built so that the things worth protecting never reach us. Your biometrics and your signing key stay on your phone, and we cannot read them, export them, or hand them to anyone.

What never leaves your device

  • Your fingerprint or face. These are read by your phone's own hardware. The app never receives them and has no way to.
  • Your passkey's private key. It is created inside your device's secure hardware and cannot be exported by this app or by us. Transactions are signed on your phone, and only the signature is sent.
  • Any seed phrase. There isn't one. Your account's authority is a passkey registered to a smart contract on Koinos.

What we store

  • Your account address and public key. Needed to build transactions for you. Both are already public on the Koinos blockchain.
  • Your passkey credential IDs — public identifiers that say which passkeys may act for your account. They cannot sign anything.
  • Conversion job records, if you use the Buy feature: the deposit addresses generated for you, amounts, and transaction hashes, kept so a conversion in progress can be finished or recovered.

What we do not collect

No name, no email address, no phone number, no contacts, no location, no photos, and no advertising or analytics identifiers. There are no third-party ad networks, trackers or analytics SDKs in this app. We do not sell or share personal data, and there is nothing here that could be sold.

The blockchain is public

Transactions you make are recorded on the Koinos blockchain, and — if you convert from Ethereum or Solana — on those networks too. Those records are public, permanent, and outside anyone's control, including ours. Anyone can see the amounts and addresses involved.

Services the app talks to

  • Koinos network nodes — to read balances and submit transactions.
  • Ethereum and Solana nodes, Jupiter, Uniswap, Wormhole and the Vortex bridge — only when you use the Buy feature, to price and carry out a conversion.
  • Price feeds — to show what your holdings are worth.

These see the network requests the app makes, including your IP address, as any website you visit would.

Funds in transit

If you convert ETH, USDC, USDT or SOL into KOIN, the money moves through addresses the app controls on your behalf until it lands as KOIN in your account. This is stated in the app before you start. Everything else you hold is controlled by your passkey and by no one else — we cannot move it, freeze it, or recover it.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Deleting your data

Email support@usekoinos.com from the address concerned, or with your account address, and we will delete the records we hold. What is already on a blockchain cannot be deleted by anyone — that is a property of the network, not a choice we make.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date above and note the change in the app's release notes.
